What the words mean every term used above
Build-up run — run about 25 walking steps, starting at a jog and getting gradually faster, finishing near full speed at the very end. Then walk back. It wakes your legs up without tiring them. Three or four of these is a warm-up.
Walk back — after a sprint, walk slowly back to where you started. That walk is the rest — don't jog it, don't rush it. It takes about 20–25 seconds, and the timer counts the remainder.
Flat out / all out — as fast as you can physically run. There is no faster.
Fast but not flat out — the speed you'd run for a bus you know you'll catch. Quick, controlled, and you could go faster if you had to. This is what "90%" means and I should have just written this.
rpm — the number on the bike's screen showing how fast you're pedalling. 60 is a gentle spin, 75 is steady, 95–100 is fast. Go by this number, not the resistance number — resistance scales are different on every bike, rpm is the same everywhere.
Incline — the percentage on the treadmill. 0% is flat, 1% is what running outdoors feels like, 6% is a decent hill you'd walk rather than run.
Measuring 20 m and 30 m — don't. Pace it out in normal walking steps: 25 steps ≈ 20 m, 40 steps ≈ 30 m. Drop your bottle at one end and your bag at the other. The exact distance doesn't matter as long as it's the same every session.
Leg swings — hold a wall or post, swing one leg forwards and backwards like a pendulum, relaxed, 10 times. Then the other leg. That's it.
Cool down — 5 minutes of easy walking at the end so you're not stopping dead from full effort.
Rules for these 17 days
Never sprint cold. The warm-up on sprint days isn't optional — cold hamstrings tear, and a pulled hamstring on the 20th means no match on the 26th. Five easy minutes, leg swings, then three build-up runs — all defined just below.
Don't add sessions. You can't build much fitness in 17 days, but you can absolutely arrive tired. Extra work now costs you the match.
The last hard day is Wednesday the 23rd. Thursday and Friday are rest, on purpose. Fitness shows up when you back off — that's when the work you've done actually lands.
Keep eating. You're still trying to gain weight, and this burns extra. On hard days add roughly 300 kcal — an extra scoop and a spoon of peanut butter in the smoothie covers it.
Still lifting? Keep Day A and Day B only, on days marked easy, and lift first, cardio after. Skip Day C until the 27th. You will not lose muscle in 17 days.
Sore is fine, sharp is not. Muscle soreness that eases as you warm up is normal. A sharp catch in the hamstring or groin means stop that session — no exceptions this close to a match.
